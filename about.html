About SeaGuard

Our Vision

SeaGuard is a coastal weather monitoring and safety awareness platform designed to make weather and marine forecast information easier to understand.

Our Objectives

Weather Monitoring

Display current weather and forecast information.

Marine Monitoring

Present available marine forecast information.

Safety Awareness

Promote awareness of official weather warnings.

Technologies Used

HTML, CSS, JavaScript and Open-Meteo API.
